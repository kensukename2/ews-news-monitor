How the status is determined. Every week, each new AI News article is analysed once by the Claude API, which lists the AI products or services it mentions, their developer, and which institutions use them at which stage (operational, testing, research, planned). Per product, the most advanced stage wins: 1 operational at an NMHS or WMO centre; 2 tested or pre-operational at an NMHS or WMO centre; 4 released (or used by companies, universities or others) but not yet tested at an NMHS or WMO centre; 3 research or prototype with real-world potential. Institutions in bold are NMHSs or WMO centres. The assessment text is generated from the linked articles only and may contain errors; please check the sources.
